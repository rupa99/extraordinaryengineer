<p>If you worked twelve hours a day this week,<br>
and you worked the weekends too,<br>
and you gave up your hobbies,<br>
and you stopped going to the gym,<br>
and you couldn't sleep at night,<br>
and you could barely wake up,<br>
and you ate lunch for dinner and dinner for breakfast,<br>
and you almost got sick at your desk,<br>
and you went straight to bed the moment you got home,<br>
and your head kept spinning,<br>
and your throat kept closing,<br>
and you got short with your parents,<br>
and you rushed through every conversation,<br>
and you turned on yourself,<br>
and your mind went numb,<br>
and you finally took a day off,<br>
and you felt like you let the team down,<br>
and you felt like you let yourself down,<br>
and you felt like you gave the best parts of yourself away,<br>
and you felt empty.</p>

<p>then I feel you.</p>

<p>You are doing something most people will never be able to relate to,<br>
and spending yourself on others is a real and rare kind of love.<br>
I see you. I love you.</p>

<p>Nothing about building a startup is natural.<br>
Teams are reaching for supernatural outcomes with supernatural tools.<br>
Accelerating into change, remaking the world, chasing what refuses to slow down.<br>
Of course it stirs up all of this in you, and more.</p>

<p>Outside of this, you are a completely normal person.<br>
Take the days you need to live like one.<br>
Slow the time down, quiet your head, and find the clarity waiting underneath.</p>

<p>I'm in this with you.</p>
